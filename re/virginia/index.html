Virginia Real Estate Exam FAQ

How many questions are on the Virginia real estate exam?

National has 80 items in 105 minutes and you need 56 correct. State has 40 items in 45 minutes and you need 30 correct. You must pass both portions.

What if I fail only one portion of the Virginia exam?

You retake only that portion. Your license application must still be in within one year of your first exam date, or the expired portion has to be taken again.

Can I take the Virginia exam from home?

Yes. You can choose a PSI test center or a remote proctored exam. Breaks are not allowed during remote proctored exams.
