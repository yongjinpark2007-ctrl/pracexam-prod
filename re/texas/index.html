Sample Texas Question

Under a TREC one-to-four family resale contract, a buyer delivers the earnest money and the option fee to the escrow agent on time. The buyer then gives proper notice of termination within the option period. What happens to the two payments?

  1. AThe seller gets the option fee, the buyer the earnest money.
  2. BThe buyer gets back both the option fee and the earnest money.
  3. CThe seller keeps both the option fee and the earnest money.
  4. DThe escrow agent holds both until a court order.
Show answer and explanation

The correct answer is A. This question asks where the option fee and earnest money end up after a timely option termination. Under the current TREC resale contract (TREC 20-18), the buyer delivers both the earnest money and the option fee to the escrow agent, made payable to the escrow agent. The buyer authorizes the escrow agent to release the option fee to the seller at any time. If the buyer gives notice of termination on time, the option fee is not refunded and any of it left with the escrow agent goes to the seller, while the earnest money is refunded to the buyer. The option fee pays for the right to walk away, so using that right does not bring it back. Earnest money, by contrast, backs the buyer’s performance of the contract, so it goes back to the buyer after a timely option termination. This buyer paid on time and terminated within the option period, so the seller gets the option fee and the buyer gets the earnest money back. B: Students who treat both payments as deposits pick this. The option fee is not refunded after termination. C: A timely option termination returns the earnest money to the buyer, so the seller does not keep it. D: The contract tells the escrow agent where each payment goes. No court order is needed.

Exam tip
On the current TREC resale contract, both the earnest money and the option fee go to the escrow agent. After that they part ways. The option fee is released to the seller and is not refunded if the buyer terminates in time, and the earnest money is refunded to the buyer. If the sale closes, the option fee is credited to the sales price. An option that says the buyer pays the fee to the seller describes an older form.

Texas Real Estate Exam FAQ

How many questions are on the Texas real estate exam?

The National portion scores 80 of 85 items and the State portion scores 40 of 50. You need 56 correct on National and 28 correct on State.

What if I fail only one portion of the Texas exam?

The portions are scored separately, so you retake only the one you failed. A passed portion stays valid for one year from the date you passed.

How many times can I take the Texas exam?

TREC says that after three failures of either exam, you must complete additional education before testing again. Your application expires one year after it is filed.
