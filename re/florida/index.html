Sample Florida Question

A buyer tells a transaction broker that a need to live closer to family is driving the purchase. The seller asks the broker why the buyer wants the home. The buyer has not waived confidentiality in writing. How should the broker respond?

  1. AReveal the motive during price negotiations
  2. BReveal the motive with oral permission alone
  3. CShare the motive with both parties equally
  4. DKeep the buyer's motivation confidential
Show answer and explanation

The correct answer is D. This question asks whether the buyer's purchasing motivation may be disclosed. A transaction broker does not disclose a party's motivation for buying or selling unless that party waives confidentiality in writing. This protects the reason for the transaction from becoming negotiating information for the other side. The desire to live near family is a purchasing motivation and remains confidential. A: This assumes the motive may be disclosed during negotiations. Negotiations alone do not end confidentiality. B: This treats oral permission as equivalent to a written waiver. The rule requires a written waiver. C: This assumes equal sharing with both sides makes disclosure permissible. Protected motivation cannot be disclosed without a written waiver merely because both sides receive it.

Exam tip
A question about why someone buys or sells may test confidentiality even without a price figure. Distinguish transaction motivation from important facts about the home itself. Remember the absence of the party's written waiver. Ordinary reasons involving family, moving, or personal circumstances may tempt you to treat the information as harmless conversation. Rather than evaluating the seriousness of the reason, ask whether it explains the party's motivation.

Florida Real Estate Exam FAQ

How many questions are on the Florida real estate exam?

The sales associate exam has 100 multiple-choice questions in 3.5 hours, and you pass with 75 points or higher.

What do I bring to the Florida exam?

Bring two forms of signature ID, one government issued, and your course completion certificate. Without the certificate you will not be admitted.

Is post-licensing education required in Florida?

Yes. You must finish 45 hours of post-licensing education before your first renewal, or the license becomes null and void.
