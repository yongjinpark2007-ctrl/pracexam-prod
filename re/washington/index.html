Sample Washington State Question

What must a buyer's agent advise when a transaction matter is beyond the agent's expertise?

  1. ASeek expert advice
  2. BRely on ordinary marketing judgment
  3. CAccept the seller's interpretation
  4. DWait until services have ended
Show answer and explanation

The correct answer is A. This question asks how an agent should respond to a transaction matter outside the agent's expertise. A buyer's agent must advise the principal to seek expert advice. The rule does not require the broker to personally solve every specialized transaction problem. Recognizing a limit in expertise is different from failing to assist the principal. Directing the principal toward expert advice supports a decision based on appropriate knowledge. Substituting ordinary brokerage experience for expertise can give the principal an unjustified sense of certainty. The question expressly establishes that the matter is beyond the agent's expertise. The required advice is therefore to seek expert assistance rather than rely on an unsuitable substitute. B: An examinee may assume general brokerage experience is sufficient for most questions. Ordinary marketing judgment does not replace the required expert-advice recommendation here. C: An examinee may think accepting the seller's explanation resolves the uncertainty. The seller's interpretation does not perform the agent's stated duty. D: An examinee may postpone a difficult issue until representation ends. The rule provides no such postponement of advice about the transaction.

Exam tip
Beyond the agent's expertise should trigger the distinction between personally resolving the issue and advising the principal to seek expert advice. Remember the condition: the matter exceeds the agent's expertise. Ordinary marketing judgment substitutes familiar experience for specialized knowledge. Accepting the seller's interpretation also fails to provide the required advice, and waiting until services end merely postpones it. Recommending expert assistance is not abandoning the principal. Choose the response that respects the stated boundary of expertise.

Washington State Real Estate Exam FAQ

How many questions are on the Washington State real estate exam?

National has 100 items in 150 minutes and State has 30 items in 90 minutes. You need a scaled score of 70 on each portion.

Why is the first Washington State license called a broker license?

Washington State has no separate salesperson license. The first license is the broker license, and the managing broker license sits above it.

What if I fail only one portion of the Washington State exam?

You retake only that portion, but you must pass it within six months of passing the other portion.
