Sample New Jersey Question

A broker-prepared standard contract for a two-family home is signed and delivered by both parties. On the next business day, the seller receives a higher offer from another buyer and wants to end the first contract. During attorney review, what is the BEST way for the seller to terminate the first contract?

  1. AWritten disapproval by the seller’s attorney
  2. BWritten cancellation sent by the listing agent
  3. CAcceptance of the other buyer’s higher offer
  4. DAn oral withdrawal by the seller
Show answer and explanation

The correct answer is A. A standard residential sales contract prepared by a New Jersey broker is subject to an attorney-review provision and, generally in transactions involving one- to four-family homes, does not become immediately final even after both parties sign and the contract is delivered. If either party's attorney disapproves the contract in writing within 3 business days, the contract may terminate before becoming binding. The seller cannot simply disregard the contract because the seller received a higher offer, but choice A, which provides for the seller to have an attorney deliver a lawful written disapproval during the review period, is a possible procedure. Choice B is incorrect because the listing agent cannot make a disapproval under the attorney-review provision on the seller's behalf. Choice C is incorrect because accepting a subsequent offer while disregarding the existing contract may create a risk of a contract dispute, and the oral withdrawal in choice D is incorrect because it does not satisfy the required written disapproval by an attorney.

Exam tip
For New Jersey attorney review questions, ask who ends the contract, how, and when. A party's attorney must deliver written disapproval within 3 business days, counted in business days, not calendar days. An oral withdrawal or a text from the seller or broker, or accepting another offer, does not replace a written disapproval. An offer received during attorney review is not a backup offer.

New Jersey Real Estate Exam FAQ

How many questions are on the New Jersey real estate exam?

The salesperson exam has 110 questions in 4 hours, and you pass with 70%, or 77 correct. Another 5 to 10 unscored questions may be added, and they count against your time.

How much is the New Jersey real estate exam?

PSI lists the exam fee as $38. The $160 license application fee is separate.

When can I retake the New Jersey exam?

PSI explains that a candidate who fails on a Wednesday can call on Thursday and may retest as soon as Friday, depending on space.
